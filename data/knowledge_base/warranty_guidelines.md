# Standard Warranty Guidelines and Consumer Rights

## 1. Warranty Classification
- **Standard Manufacturer Warranty:**
  - Typically 12 months from the date of purchase (or invoice date).
  - Covers manufacturing defects, component failures, and circuit malfunctions occurring during regular, intended use.
- **Seller Warranty:**
  - Provided directly by the merchant/reseller (common in refurbished or imported electronics).
  - Binding upon the merchant irrespective of manufacturer recognition.
- **Extended Warranty / Care Packs:**
  - Purchased service contracts that begin immediately after the manufacturer warranty lapses.

## 2. Standard Exclusions & Voiding Conditions
A manufacturer or service provider may legally decline a warranty claim under explicit exclusion clauses:
1. **Physical & Accidental Damage:** Cracks on screens, bent casings, severed cables caused by drops, impacts, or undue force.
2. **Liquid / Moisture Damage:** Contact with water or corrosive liquids, unless product carries certified IP ingress protection and was used strictly within rated immersion limits.
3. **Unauthorized Modification:** Opening, repairing, or altering hardware components by unauthorized third-party technicians.
4. **Altered / Erased Serial Numbers:** Tampered IMEI, serial barcodes, or warranty seal stickers.
5. **Normal Wear and Tear:** Gradual battery capacity degradation, minor cosmetic scratches that do not impede normal device operation.

## 3. Statutory Consumer Rights during Warranty
Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Sections 84-86 Product Liability):
- The manufacturer is liable to repair or replace a defective product within a reasonable timeframe (standard industry turnaround: 14 to 21 working days).
- If the authorized service center fails to repair the defect after multiple attempts (repeat failures) or if replacement parts are permanently unavailable, the consumer is entitled to either:
  1. A brand new replacement unit of equivalent or higher specifications; or
  2. A commercial refund subject to lawful statutory depreciation.
- Blanket rejections without a technical inspection job-sheet are deemed a "deficiency in service".
