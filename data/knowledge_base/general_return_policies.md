# Benchmark Platform Return, Replacement & Refund Policies

## 1. Electronics & Consumer Durables
- **Replacement Window:** 7 days from the confirmed date of delivery (Amazon India / Flipkart standard policy).
- **Condition for Return/Replacement:**
  - Product must be in original condition with un-tampered brand outer box, MRP tags attached, user manual, warranty cards, CDs, and all accessories.
  - For mobile phones, laptops, and smart wearables: Service center inspection or technician verification may be required prior to refund issuance.
- **Refund Policy for Defective Electronics:**
  - If a replacement unit is unavailable in stock, the marketplace or seller must issue a 100% full refund of the invoice amount.
  - Return turnaround: Pickup within 2–4 business days of approval; refund credited within 3–5 business days to the original payment method.

## 2. Fashion & Lifestyle
- **Return / Exchange Window:** 10 to 14 days from delivery.
- **Exceptions:** Innerwear, swimwear, socks, and personal hygiene items are non-returnable once the package seal is broken, except for verified delivery of incorrect or counterfeit items.

## 3. Large Appliances (TVs, Refrigerators, Washing Machines)
- **Policy:** 10-day replacement policy in case of physical damage during transit, missing parts, or dead on arrival (DOA).
- **Mandatory Procedure:**
  - Brand authorized installation is typically required. Self-installation or opening of sealed boxes by unauthorized technicians can void transit damage claims unless explicitly documented in unboxing video.

## 4. Cancellation & Pre-Dispatch Refunds
- Where an order is cancelled prior to shipment dispatch, 100% refund must be credited within 24–48 hours for UPI/Debit Card payments, and 3–7 business days for Net Banking / Credit Cards.
- Restocking fees or arbitrary processing deductions are prohibited under Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules for cancelled or defective deliveries.
